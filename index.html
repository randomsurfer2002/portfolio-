03 // PROJECT_01: SMART TABLET BOX

IoT MEDICATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Designed to eliminate medication non-adherence through visual haptics and real-time digital feedback. Features a 7x3 matrix covering a full weekly cycle (Morning, Afternoon, Night) with high-contrast backlit compartments.

  • Temporal Logic Matrix: 7-Day, 3-Tier Grid (M/A/N) layout for instant regimen status.
  • Chromatic Cues: Color-coded backlighting (Warm/Cool/Neutral) for dosage differentiation in low light.
  • Information-Rich HUD: Front OLED display showing environmental context (25°C, humidity) affecting medication.
  • Connectivity Stack: Bluetooth & Wi-Fi sync for automated caregiver notifications.

04 // PROJECT_02: VAYURA

DUAL-PURPOSE ATMOSPHERIC DEVICE

Merges a high-performance Wi-Fi 6 router with a smart scent-diffusion system. Utilizes active waste heat generated by the router's internal PCB to facilitate essential oil diffusion through a borosilicate glass column.

  • Thermal Scavenging: Engineered airflow channels heat from electronics directly into scent diffusion.
  • Material Contrast: Organic wood-grain base balanced against a precision monolithic spire.
  • Circular Haptics: Base LED ring provides non-intrusive ambient connectivity and oil-level alerts.
  • Modular Encapsulation: Quick-swap glass capsules enable maintenance without cross-contamination.
